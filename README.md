# 2026-Optimization-Guide
List of performance mods for mainstream versions (1.21.1, 1.20.1) which groups mods on what they do and lists what performance mods are incompatible (does the same thing) and compatible with their own niches.

This guide will cover a large list of performance mods, which for people who want to compare how they would work against others. I will also provide libraries/groups of mods that target specific parts of your machine. e.g, CPU>Terrain Generation or RAM>GC Optimization. Hopefully, this can help people and bring awareness to lesser-known but affective performance mods.
